When decomposing a task, consider previously performed tasks and how they changed object state and position.

If an object's position is known from short-term memory, prefer GoToObject_with_memory(robot, 'ObjectType') or GoToObject_next_time(robot, 'ObjectType') instead of Explore().

If object's position changed after prior tasks, use GoToObject() to the remembered coordinates rather than re-exploring the whole scene.
